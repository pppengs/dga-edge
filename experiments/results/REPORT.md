# DGA method validation report

Generated by `experiments/run_validation.py`.

## Metrics

- `decision_rate` - share of samples the method was willing to judge.
- `acc_of_decided` - accuracy counted only over decided samples.
- `acc_of_all` - accuracy over every sample, undecided counted as wrong.
  These two differ a lot and must be reported together; quoting only
  one of them is the most common way DGA comparisons mislead.

## Caveats

- Labels are the datasets' own published annotations, not independent
  physical inspection. They are treated as ground truth for comparison only.
- `中低温过热` in the Cliango dataset merges two IEC thermal tiers and is
  mapped to T1, which is a lossy mapping.
- The IEC three-ratio coded variant returns ND for code triples that the
  literature documents inconsistently (see dga_core/tables.py).
